Indonesian rulings

What Muhammadiyah and NU say about crypto

Indonesia's two largest Islamic organisations have both revised their position. Each now accepts crypto as a digital asset that can be held and traded, while forbidding its use as currency in Indonesia. Earlier rulings from 2021–2022 had declared it haram.

Timeline

From prohibition to conditional permission.

  1. Oct 2021
    PWNU East Java (Nahdlatul Ulama): haram
    Its bahtsul masail found crypto didn't meet the conditions of a valid sale and leaned towards fraud and gambling.
  2. Nov 2021
    MUI: haram as currency
    Indonesian Ulema Council ruling on cryptocurrency as money.
  3. Jan 2022
    Muhammadiyah (Majelis Tarjih): haram
    Haram both as an investment and as a medium of exchange, citing gharar and maysir.
  4. 4 Mar 2026
    Muhammadiyah: permissible with conditions
    New fatwa on crypto as a digital financial asset: permissible with conditions on the coin and on how it is traded.
  5. 29 Aug 2026
    35th NU Muktamar: Bitcoin is an asset
    Bahtsul Masail Waqi'iyah: Bitcoin is mal and a valid medium of exchange; using it as currency is forbidden.
  6. Expected Nov 2026
    DSN-MUI fatwa on Bitcoin
    The National Sharia Council said it is studying Bitcoin and targeting a fatwa this month.
Mubah muqayyad

Muhammadiyah

Majelis Tarjih dan Tajdid PP Muhammadiyah · Fatwa on crypto as a digital financial asset · Yogyakarta, 15 Ramadan 1447 H / 4 March 2026 · signed by Dr. H. Hamim Ilyas (Chair) and M. Rofiq Muzakkir (Secretary)
  • ✓A valuable asset (māl mutaqawwam). Crypto has utility people want, can be stored in a digital wallet and has economic value recognised by custom ('urf).
  • ✓Trading and investing are permissible in principle, following the rule that dealings are allowed until evidence shows otherwise.

Conditions on the coin itself (permission is conditional, muqayyad):

  • ✕No use in haram activity: crypto gambling, pornography, dark-web markets (Qur'an 5:2).
  • ✕Must have real utility, such as store of value, platform access or governance, or Web3 infrastructure. Meme coins and pure-speculation tokens are haram, as waste (tabdhīr), maysir and gharar.
  • ✕No Ponzi or pyramid schemes, where returns come from new investors rather than real value.

Conditions on how you trade:

  • ✕No futures: both sides are deferred, a sale of debt for debt.
  • ✕No leverage or margin: the loan carries interest (riba) and ties a loan to a sale.
  • ✕No short selling, on futures or spot (borrowed coins carry interest or funding fees).
  • ✕No interest-bearing lending or fixed / guaranteed yields on coins you hand over.
  • ✕No pump-and-dump or other market manipulation (najsh).

Other rulings:

  • !Airdrops are permissible as a gift or reward, unless earning one means promoting gambling, spreading hoaxes or fake reviews, or locking funds the operator puts to use.
  • !Know before you trade: learn the basics and do your own research, and use only exchanges registered with and supervised by OJK.
  • ✕Not a currency. Extreme volatility, fixed supply and state monetary authority mean crypto can't serve as money; paying with it in Indonesia is not lawful.
The fatwa calls itself a dynamic guideline, not an invitation to invest, and warns against speculative euphoria. Full fatwa text (PDF, 11 pages) Announcement · muhammadiyah.or.id Majelis Tarjih · tarjih.or.id
Asset: valid · Currency: haram

Nahdlatul Ulama

Bahtsul Masail Waqi'iyah, 35th NU Muktamar · Tambakberas, Jombang · 29 August 2026
  • ✓Bitcoin is māl (property) under the criteria of fiqh.
  • ✓Bitcoin can be tsaman, a medium of exchange, in fiqh terms.
  • ✓Transacting in Bitcoin as a digital asset is valid and permitted.
  • ✕As currency: "sah tapi haram", valid in principle but forbidden, because it conflicts with Law No. 7/2011 on Currency.
  • !Reasoning: it has real, permissible benefit and customary value ('urf), can be transferred between owners, is protected by its private key, and follows OJK's treatment of crypto as a digital asset.
Announced by KH Mahbub Maafi, Chair of LBM PBNU. The ruling addresses Bitcoin specifically and says nothing on leverage or other tokens. NU Online, 30 Aug 2026

How we screen

One set of rules for every coin

Each coin is judged by what its project actually does, against the core prohibitions of Islamic commercial law (fiqh al-mu'amalat). A coin is marked Halal only when its main activity is permissible and stays clear of the three things Shariah forbids in a financial instrument.

01

Riba

interest

Projects that mainly earn from interest: lending and borrowing at interest, fixed or guaranteed yields, and yield-bearing dollars backed by debt or funding rates.

Qur'an 2:275
02

Maysir

gambling

Tokens built for betting, casinos, prediction markets or games of chance, where one side's gain comes from another's loss on an uncertain outcome.

Qur'an 5:90
03

Gharar

excessive uncertainty

Projects whose value or mechanics are unclear or hidden, and assets bought on hype alone. These aren't banned outright but are treated with caution.

Sahih Muslim 1513

The three verdicts

Coins that don't clearly pass aren't dropped from the list. They are marked so you can decide with the full picture.

Halal

The project's main use is permissible: blockchains, infrastructure, payments, spot exchanges, games. Score 85/100.

Doubtful

Scholars disagree, or the project mixes permissible and doubtful activity: stablecoins, staking, DeFi, meme coins, privacy coins, tokenised stocks. Score 50/100.

Not Halal

The project's core is interest, leverage or gambling: lending protocols, perpetual-futures exchanges, tokenised debt, betting. Score 0/100.

This is a screening tool, not a fatwa. Verdicts follow HalalScreener's category rulings, and coins it hasn't rated are estimated with the same rules. Always do your own research and ask a qualified scholar before making a financial decision.

Frequently asked

Halal crypto questions

Is crypto trading halal?

Many contemporary scholars allow spot buying and selling of cryptocurrencies whose underlying project is permissible, as long as the trade settles immediately and you own what you bought.

Leverage, margin, futures, options and short selling are generally not permitted, because they involve interest, debt or selling what you don't own. This applies whatever coin you trade.

Is Bitcoin halal?

Most screens, including HalalScreener, rate Bitcoin Halal. It is a decentralised network for holding and sending value, and nothing in its protocol pays interest or involves gambling.

A minority of scholars consider all cryptocurrencies impermissible, mainly because of volatility and the lack of an issuer. This screen follows the majority view but you should know the debate exists.

Are exchanges and exchange tokens halal?

Using an exchange for spot trading in permissible coins is generally accepted. Avoid futures, margin, lending, "earn" products with a fixed yield and any product that pays interest.

Exchange tokens such as BNB or OKB are rated Halal here because their main use is fee discounts and access. Stricter scholars look through to the exchange's whole business, including its derivatives desk, and may disagree.

Is staking halal?

It depends on the mechanism. Rewards for helping secure a network (proof of stake) are seen by many scholars as payment for work, but some liken them to interest. That is why liquid staking and restaking are rated Doubtful.

Products that lend your coins out or promise a fixed return are treated as interest and rated Not Halal.

Are stablecoins halal?

Stablecoins are rated Doubtful. They are useful for settling trades, but most issuers earn interest on the reserves backing them, and some keep their peg through lending. Yield-bearing dollars that pay you interest or funding-rate income are rated Not Halal.

What do Muhammadiyah and NU say about crypto?

Both changed their earlier haram rulings in 2026. Muhammadiyah (March 2026) treats crypto as a valuable asset that may be traded and invested in, with conditions: the coin needs real utility (meme coins are haram) and must not be tied to gambling or Ponzi schemes, and futures, margin, short selling, interest-bearing lending and using crypto as currency are forbidden. NU's 35th Muktamar (August 2026) ruled Bitcoin a valid asset to transact, but forbade using it as currency.

What do "Estimate" and "Unrated" mean?

HalalScreener hasn't rated every coin. For a top-500 coin it doesn't cover, the verdict marked Estimate applies the rule of the HalalScreener category the project most resembles, with the reason shown under its category. It is not a HalalScreener verdict.

Unrated means there wasn't enough public information about the project to place it. Treat these with extra care.
