About this screen

Category verdicts and coin lists come from the category pages of HalalScreener, read on 17 November 2026. The site also lists coins not yet assigned to a category; those aren't shown in the Category tab. Verdicts cover spot buying and holding only: margin, leverage, futures and fixed-yield lending fail regardless of the coin.

Top 500 coins, prices, market caps and FDV (fully diluted valuation) come from CoinMarketCap and are refreshed automatically about every 20 minutes. Coins are matched to HalalScreener by CoinMarketCap id where known, otherwise by ticker. Rows marked "Estimate" apply the rule of the HalalScreener category each coin most resembles; they are not HalalScreener verdicts. "Unrated" means there wasn't enough public information to place the coin.

AAOIFI has no cryptocurrency standard, so HalalScreener screens against published scholarly opinion and calls itself a screen, not a fatwa. Scholars who consider all crypto impermissible would not accept any coin listed here. Check with a scholar you trust before relying on any verdict.
